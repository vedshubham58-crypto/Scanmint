# ScanMint privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

ScanMint ("the app") is a document scanner for Android made by an independent developer
(vedshubham). This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Your documents stay on your phone

- Pages you scan, pictures and PDFs you import, text read from pages, and your signature are
  stored **only in the app's private storage on your phone**. We do not upload them, and we have
  no server that could receive them.
- Scanning and text reading run on your phone through Google Play services (Google ML Kit).
  Google does not receive your pages for this.
- A document leaves your phone only when **you** choose Share or Save to phone, and then it goes
  only where you send it.
- Password-locked PDFs: the password is used once to lock the copy you share. It is not stored.
- If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's files in your own Google account
  backup. You control this in your phone's settings.

## Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google may collect
your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device
and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland the app asks
for your consent first (Google's consent form) and you can change your choice any time under
**Go Pro → Ad choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
See how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Pro subscribers see no ads.

## Payments

ScanMint Pro is bought through **Google Play**. Google handles the payment; we never see your card
or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active.
Cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

## Permissions

ScanMint asks for internet access (for ads and the Play subscription check). It does not ask for
access to your camera roll, contacts or location: the camera opens inside Google's scanner, and
you pick each picture or file yourself.

## Children

ScanMint is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. We hold no
copy on any server, so there is nothing else to delete.
